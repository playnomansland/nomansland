# B1: Tree kojja and Scorch turrets (2v2 buildings)

## Patch notes

**Tree kojja** (2v2 building, defensive): a small watchtower grown into a tree. It shoots like the Outpost, reaches 20 m and sees stealthed units in that range.
- *Grow another* (100, once a round for the team): whoever buys it places a new kojja within 3 tiles of one of your kojjas. You place it yourself, with no vote and no 2-tile room needed. Once someone has bought a skill on the new kojja, Grow can't be taken back.
- *Nest* (50, once per kojja): the kojja holds one ranged squad. It can be yours or your teammate's: place it on the kojja or move it in. Only one body shoots from inside, with +3 m range and stealth sight. After 10 s without seeing an enemy, or when the kojja falls, the whole squad steps out and fights on foot. Nest can't be taken back while a squad is in it.
- Downside: a kojja destroyed in a fight stands for the other team the next round. It shoots for them, and they can use its Nest (or buy one) and put a ranged squad of theirs in it, even though it is on your board. The round after, it is yours again. A squad standing in a kojja when it changes hands steps out to the nearest free tile of its own zone.

**Scorch turrets** (2v2 building, defensive): each of you places your own turret, whenever you like (neither of you waits for the other). A turret is one Ember Imp with Spitfire (burn), instant shots and +10 m range, and it always sees stealthed units. It has 450 health and 2 armor. It isn't a unit, so the other team doesn't need to destroy it to win the round.
- *Blaze* (100): splash 5 m and +25% hit. *Rapid fire* (100): +25% attack speed and +5 m range. *Legs* (100): the turret walks and counts as a unit. Each turret has its own skills, and either of you can buy them.
- Downside: a turret destroyed in a fight sits out the next fight and loses all its skills.

## README lines

- Tree kojja (`treeKojja` region of src/mpb.js, `KOJJA`): fight unit `Mpb.kojjaUnit()` (Outpost hit/aps, range 20, `trueSight`, structure). Place rule `hooks.spotOk`: the first kojja is interior and spaced, and a grown one is within `KOJJA.grow` (Chebyshev) of an own standing kojja, in the own zone, with no spacing (`place.where: 'own', spacing: false`). Grow queues a one-player placement (`Mpb.queue(..., lane)`), and taking it back drops the queue entry or marks the placed kojja `rec.away` (`data.undone`). Nest: `hooks.holds` makes `Game.garrisonFree`/`garrisonAt` (via `Mpb.holds` → `Game.mpbHolds`) accept one ranged squad (fight range > `KOJJA.ranged` = Engine melee reach 5) on the tile. `hooks.squadBodies` tags its bodies (`b.nestOf`), and `hooks.link` (called by `Mpb.link` from `Duo.battle` once all bodies are in) puts one body `inside` the kojja with `b.nest = { range, sight }`, +3 m and `trueSight`. The other bodies are boarded riders of that body. Engine `unnest(b)` lets them all out, with the range restored, when it leaves (WATCH_IDLE or the kojja falls).
- Kojja downside: at `roundStart` after `rec.fell === round - 1`, the kojja gets `rec.away = true` and `data.turned = round`. A stand-in record in the opponents' list (`data.ghost = <key>`, reused for every later turn, carrying the owners' Nest as an old buy) takes its tile and body for that round. The next round the stand-in is `away` and the kojja is back. Squads on the tile are evicted to the nearest free own-zone tile (both lanes' armies, deterministic ring search). A record that is `away` can't buy skills.
- Scorch turrets (`scorch` region, `SCORCH`): `hooks.acquire` queues two one-player placements (lane 0, lane 1), and `rec.data.lane` records whose turret it is. `Mpb.scorchUnit(m, rec)` builds the match's Ember Imps with their burn skill applied as one body: hp 450, armor 2, `projSpeed: 0`, range +10, `trueSight` and `scorchSight` (Engine: sees stealth at any distance), `structure`/`lesser`. Blaze, Rapid fire and Legs are `per: 'building'`. Legs leaves out `structure`, uses the imps' speed, sets `mpbSkin: 'mobile'` and gets `b.tier` from the imps. After `afterFight` with `rec.fell === round`, `rec.skills = {}` and `data.out = round + 1`, and `bodies` returns `[]` that round.
- Framework additions (small, default-off; when no kind uses them, nothing changes; also written into docs/MPB-2V2.md, Hooks, Placement vote and "Adding a kind"):
  - `hooks.canUndo(m, side, rec, sk, lane)` (consulted by `Mpb.canUndo`): the kojja refuses Nest while a squad stands in it (as Overwatch in game.js) and Grow once the grown kojja has a skill click this round.
  - Undo all (`Duo.rebuildTeam`): the player's own `mpbBuy`/`mpbUndo` entries are replayed in place, then taken back latest first through `Mpb.canUndo`; what can't be taken back stays bought and its cost is charged again to that player's lane (their gold is back at the round's start). Records never vanish on a replay, so keys don't shift (a grown kojja stays as `away`/`data.undone`), and the teammate's buys land on the same records.
  - `Mpb.voteEntry(m, side, lane)` / `Mpb.placeEntry`: a one-player placement anywhere in the leading run of one-player entries can be placed by its own player (Scorch turrets in either order). `Duo.mpbBots` and the page's vote box, glowing tiles and tap use it; `Duo.mpbClock` keys a one-player head by its lane, so the teammate's placement doesn't restart its 15 s.
  - `Mpb.hide`: queue entries with `skill: true` (the kojja's Grow sets it) are dropped from the opponents' view, and a kind's `hide` returning `false` drops the record (the kojja: one grown and taken back, `data.undone`).
  - `hooks.fellText(m, side, rec)`: the sheet's "destroyed in round N" line (the page's generic line said "it stands again in every fight", wrong for both B1 kinds).
  - render3d: a squad in a kojja's Nest stands on the kojja's roof on the deploy board (`Mpb.recAt` → `Mpb.unitOf`), as in the fight.
  - `rec.away`: no tiles (`tilesOf`), no body, holds nothing.
  - The `lane` argument of `Mpb.queue`: one player places it, at once, with no vote. `mpbVote` refuses the other lane, `placeHead` sets `rec.data.lane`, `Duo.mpbBots` skips the other lane's bot, and the page's vote box, glowing tiles and tap follow the same rule.
  - Three new hooks, each with its framework call:
    - `hooks.holds` with `Mpb.holds`.
    - `hooks.link` with `Mpb.link`, called by `Duo.battle`.
    - `hooks.bot(m, side, rec, r)` with `Mpb.botTurn`, called from `Duo.botTurn` before `aiDeploy` with a seeded rng.
- Engine (default-off body/unit fields): `u.scorchSight` in `seesHidden`, and `b.nest` (`unnest`, plus the carrier-death release). Game: `mpbHolds` in `garrisonAt`/`garrisonFree`, which is false without `m.mpb`.
