# Group B3: Coal mine and Magic lake (2v2 buildings)

## Patch notes

- **Coal mine** (technical): your team votes for the entrance in your zone, then for the exit in no man's land. Squads deployed on the 8 tiles round the entrance go down the mine when the fight starts and come up 2 seconds later on the matching tile round the exit, then stand still for 1.5 seconds (as a squad new on a flank tile) before they fight. While they are underground nobody can see or hit them. Nobody (either team) can deploy on the 8 tiles round the exit, even where they are the other team's zone. If an enemy squad already stands on the tile a squad would come up on, that squad stays where it was. The entrance can be destroyed (squads already on their way still come up); the exit can't be hit.
  - **Dig a new exit** (150 gold, once a match): move the exit once, anywhere in no man's land or to a flank tile of the enemy's half. Tap the mine, then a glowing tile.
  - **Fresh air** (200 gold, once a match): squads coming up the mine are 25% faster and have +4 armor for 5 seconds.
- **Magic lake** (technical, terrain): each of you puts down 2 water tiles of your own on your zone or anywhere in no man's land (not on flank tiles, squads or buildings). Water is shot over but never walked on, and nobody can deploy on it. Water can never shut off a tile or a way across the board: a tile that would is not offered. A tile you put down can be moved until the fight; one you didn't put down goes on its own before the fight. Water can't be destroyed.
  - **Waterspirits allowal** (50 gold, per tile): your units can walk through that tile, 30% slower, and regain 5% health a second while on it.
  - **Become Water elemental** (200 gold, per tile): every fight a Water Elemental (as a level 2 Water Mage's Summon water elemental) rises from that tile and fights for your team. The tile stays water.
