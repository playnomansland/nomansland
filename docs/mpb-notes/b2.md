# Group B2: Field hospital and The Oak (2v2 buildings)

## Patch notes

**2v2: two support buildings, the Field hospital and The Oak** (they come as artifact cards)

- **Field hospital** (600 health, 2 armor, can't shoot): squads you or your teammate deploy on the 8 tiles round it
  get +20% health and regain 2% of their health a second in the fight. Every round your team wins with the hospital
  still standing: +5 Team HP (never above your starting 200). Downside: destroyed, −15 Team HP.
  - *Field dressing* (50 gold, +50 for each of your own clicks this round, as often as you like): a random squad of
    your team gets +25% health for this round's fight (picked when the fight starts, so nobody can tell beforehand).
  - *Second wind* (100 gold, once a round for the team): pick one of your team's squads. The first time it is wiped
    out in the fight it comes back whole, at full health, on the tile in front of the hospital. Either of you can move
    it to another squad for free once it is bought. The opponents don't see it. A squad that gives itself up on
    purpose (Ember Imps summoning their Hellfiend) wasn't wiped out: nothing comes back. A squad brought back keeps
    its Home ground, its Mark Bounty hunt and the like.
- **The Oak** (1200 health, 3 armor, can't shoot): *Restoration*: while the Oak stands, every heal and every bit of
  regeneration on your team is 25% stronger (healers, water healing, pools, healbots, lifesteal, regen...).
  Downside: the round after it was destroyed, a level 3 Ent (one body, all four of its skills) fights for the
  opponents, in front of their tower.
  - *Heal* (50 gold, once a round each): +5 Team HP, counted before this round's fight takes its damage (never above
    200). The results screen lists it.
  - *Double restoration* (100 gold, once a round for the team): Restoration is +50% this round.
  - *Sticky moss* (200 gold, once a match): every tile within 3 tiles of the Oak turns to moss for good. Enemies on it
    move 30% slower (the strongest slow counts; Berserkers can't be slowed). Both teams see the moss.
- Bots buy these skills too.

## README lines

- Field hospital (`fieldHospital` region of src/mpb.js, `FH`): `squadMods` gives squads (either lane) within
  Chebyshev 1 +20% hp and `u.regen += 2`; `afterFight`: +5 Team HP when `res.winner === side` and it didn't fall
  (capped at `TEAM_HP_CAP` = 2 × `Game.START_HP`), −15 when `rec.fell === round` (`summary.mpbNews`, kind
  `fieldHospital`). Field dressing (`dress`, 50 +50 costStep, `per: 'free'`): `battleOpts` puts one seeded number per
  click this round in `opts.mpb.fieldHospital.dress[side]` (seed: `m.fhSeed`, set by `Duo.runFight` from `D.seed`
  and the round just before the fight, so it is in the fight's `pres` but in no deploy view; `Duo.fight` deletes it
  after building the battle; falls back to `m.seed` when absent; the same numbers for both sides); the Engine gives the squad at that share of the side's fighting squads (ids ≥ 0, sorted) +25% hp.
  Second wind (`wind`, 100, `per: 'round'`): `Game.mpbBuy(m, side, key, 'wind', lane, id)` (the squad's lane and its id
  in that lane), `rec.data.wind = { round, lane, id }` (hidden from the opponents by `hide`); `Game.mpbAct(m, side, key,
  'wind', lane, id)` moves it (either teammate). `squadBodies` marks that squad's bodies `b.fhRevive = { x, y }` (the
  tile in front of the hospital); the Engine (with `opts.mpb.fieldHospital`) brings them back once, as new bodies
  (`b.fhBack`; the fallen ones `b.fhGone` no longer count in `result().squads`), copying the flags Game.bodiesFor set as
  the fight began (`FH_KEEP`: band, sniperMark, marked, prey, zoe, shroom, aura, glutN, mask). A group with a
  `b.sacrificed` body (a portal's summoning) spends the revive without firing. Page: `MPB_SHEET.fieldHospital` (the
  squad picker); the skill has `pick: true`, so the generic sheet row shows "Pick above" instead of a Buy button.
- The Oak (`oak` region, `OAK`): its body carries `b.oakRestore` (1.25, 1.5 with `boost`), `battleOpts` sets
  `opts.mpb.oak`; the Engine's `heal()` multiplies every heal of a side by the largest `oakRestore` of its bodies
  standing as the tick starts (fixed at the top of `step`, so the order of play on the tick an Oak falls doesn't
  matter). Heal (`heal`, 50, `roundPlayer`): new hook `teamHp(lanes, side, rec, hp, round, sums)`, run by
  `Mpb.teamHeal(lanes, hp, round, summaries)` from `Duo.fight` before the fight's damage comes off the Team HP; it writes
  an `mpbNews` line (kind `oak`, `healed`) with the amount actually gained. Sticky moss (`moss`, 200, `match`): `battleOpts` adds
  the 49 tiles within Chebyshev 3 to `opts.tiles` as `mud` tiles of the Oak's side (`slow: 30`, the Engine's existing
  spell-zone mud: the strongest slow, never on `unstoppable`); `Mpb.oakMossTiles(m, rec)`; drawn by
  `MPB_FLOOR.oak` (render2d: a new floor hook, so the 3D floor has it too). Downside: new hook
  `foeBodies(m, side, rec, ctx)` (`Mpb.bodies` asks the *other* team's buildings for bodies that fight for `side`;
  `ctx.xsq(j)` of that building's index): the round after `rec.fell`, `Mpb.oakEntUnit(m)` (the roster's Ent, 1 body,
  every skill in `E.SLOTS` applied) at level index 2, in front of the opponents' tower, squad `Mpb.extraOf(k, 9)`
  (a team body of lane 0; counts as a unit).
- Framework additions (default off): `Mpb.bodies` → `hooks.foeBodies`; `Mpb.teamHeal(lanes, hp, round, sums)` →
  `hooks.teamHp` (called in `Duo.fight` right before `hp -= lost`); `Mpb.bots(m, side)` → `hooks.bot(m, side, rec)`
  (called in `Duo.botTurn` after `aiDeploy`, before the Vault put). render2d: `MPB_FLOOR[kind](v, side, rec, t)` from
  `drawBoard`. Sheet: a skill's `pick: true` replaces the generic row's Buy button with "Pick above". Engine (`opts.mpb.fieldHospital`, `opts.mpb.oak`; nothing changes without them): Field dressing at
  `createBattle`, Second wind at the end of `step`, `fhGone` in `result`, the Oak's factor in `heal`.
- Tests: `node tools/tests/mpb-b2.js`.

## To check with Vilhelm

- Field hospital "1 tile range": the 8 tiles round it (Chebyshev 1), squads of both teammates; the bonus holds for the
  whole fight even after the hospital falls.
- "+5 Team HP for a round won": only when the team won the fight (a draw gives nothing); capped at the team's starting
  200. A destroyed hospital gives −15 and no +5 that round.
- Field dressing: "a random allied unit" = a random squad of either player (not the buildings, the tower or summons),
  picked when the fight starts (so squads placed after the click count too) from a seed no player holds while
  deploying (so it can't be steered); two clicks may hit the same squad (then it gets +50%). The price step (+50) is per player, as the framework does it.
- Second wind: "chosen allied unit" = the squad picked on the sheet (either player's); it works even if the hospital
  has fallen by then; it comes back as fresh bodies (full health, no burn or poison) on the tile in front of the
  hospital, once a fight. Bodies won over by a Gentleman count as gone. A squad its own portal sacrificed (Summon
  hellfiend) didn't die: Second wind is spent and does nothing.
- The Oak's Restoration only works while the Oak stands in the fight (once it falls, heals are normal again). It
  covers every heal path of the Engine (regen, healers, mending, water healing, pools, healbots, lifesteal, slime
  units drinking poison); a Mecha repairbot mending a building isn't a heal and isn't changed. "Double the
  effectiveness" read as +50% instead of +25%.
- The Oak's Heal: 5 Team HP a click, applied with the fight (before its damage), so it can't lift a team that the
  same fight beats; never above 200. The HP bar shows it after the fight, the sheet says it is booked.
- Sticky moss: "big 3 tile radius" = the 7×7 tiles round the Oak (Chebyshev 3, its own tile too); permanent; only
  the opponents are slowed; it never slows Berserkers; the strongest slow on a body counts (as mud).
- The Ent: one body, level 3 (the top level), all four Ent skills including Forward planting (which only adds +10%
  health in a fight) and both of the either/or pair; it stands in front of the opponents' tower and counts as one of
  their units for winning the round (a summon). It comes only the round right after the Oak fell (again each time
  it falls).
- The Oak's and the hospital's hp/armor are the table's numbers (1200/3 and 600/2).
