# Group B4: the Jail and the School (2v2 buildings, 6 Nov 2026)

## Patch notes

- **Jail** (2v2 building, technical; 1500 health, 8 armor). **Skywatch**: the enemy's stealthed units within 3 tiles of
  it are seen while they stand in its light (the lit tiles show on the board). **Jail a squad** (100 gold): pick an enemy
  squad from an earlier round (either opponent's); from this round's fight on it takes no part in any fight until the
  jail is destroyed. Each of you keeps one squad in it (2 cells). They find out at the fight; after that their squad
  sits in your jail (they can still sell it, which frees your cell). A horse jailed leaves its rider behind.
  **Jail guards** (150 gold, once a match): 4 Keep guards stand round the jail in every fight and hold their posts until
  an enemy comes into their awareness. **Downside**: destroyed, the jailed squads go free (back to their own zone), their
  unit types can't be jailed again this match, and the next round the jail does nothing (no jailing, no Skywatch, no
  guards).
- **School** (2v2 building, support; 700 health, 2 armor). Either of you can put one of your squads in it: it leaves the
  board and doesn't fight, and you can't place another squad of that unit while it is inside. **Study** (free): it gets a
  level's worth of XP every round (levelling up still costs gold). **Learn** (free): a skill of its unit, ready 2 rounds
  later; it can't leave meanwhile. **Take out**: free, onto the tile it came from (or the nearest free one), or a tile you
  tap. **Double study** (200, once a match): twice the XP. **Quick learning** (300, once a match): skills in 1 round.
  **Downside**: destroyed, the squad that destroyed it gets half a level's XP, and the squad inside comes out with the
  XP and level it went in with (a skill being learnt is lost). Tap the School to see the squad inside and open its sheet.

## README lines

- Jail (`jail` region of src/mpb.js, `Mpb.JAIL`): `rec.data = { cells: [{ by (buyer's lane), lane, id (the squad in its
  own lane), ui, round }], banned: [unit indices], down: round }`. Skill `jail` = `Game.mpbBuy(m, side, key, 'jail', lane,
  id)` (the target as the buyer sees it while deploying: `Game.foeSeen` in their own lane, `Game.teamSeen` for the other
  lane; `Mpb.jailWhy` / `Mpb.jailable`). This round's cells are hidden from the opponents (`hide`). In the fight a jailed
  squad has no bodies (new framework hook `foeSquadBodies`: `Mpb.squadBodies` also runs the other team's buildings over a
  squad). After the fight (`afterFight`, both lanes) the squad gets `sq.jailed = { side, key, round, col, row }` and stands
  on the jail's tile; its riders step out (`delete r.in`, both lanes). A squad sold (or put in its School) before the fight
  refunds the jailer; sold later frees its cell. Destroyed: freed onto `Mpb.homeTile` (its old tile or the nearest free
  one), types banned, `down = round + 1` (no jailing, Skywatch, guards; `litTiles` empty).
- Skywatch: `opts.mpb.discover = [{ x, y, r, side }]` (Engine, default off): a stealthed body of the other side inside the
  square |dx|, |dy| <= r is not hidden (r = 3.5 tiles: the 7 x 7 tiles round the jail). Lit tiles: new framework hook
  `lit(m, side, rec)` / `Mpb.litTiles`, drawn by render2d `drawMpbs` and the render3d MPB loop (soft amber squares); in a
  2D fight `MPB_DRAW.jail` draws the light itself.
- Jail guards: `XSQ` squad of 4 bodies, the Keep guards' unit (the roster's Wisps, base stats) named `Jail Guard`,
  `b.guard`, `b.holdT = 1e9`, `b.holdSee` (Engine, default off: a holding body stops holding once `seesFoe(b)`: an enemy it
  could pick within range + sight, at least the minimum sight, in sight).
- School (`school` region, `Mpb.SCHOOL`): `rec.data = { pupil: { lane, id, ui, round, learn: { k, round } | null, sq },
  was: [this round's departures] }`, the squad `sq.school = { key, round, col, row, xp, level }` on the school's tile.
  Actions `Game.mpbAct(m, side, key, 'enroll', id | 'leave' [, col, row] | 'learn', k | 'unlearn')` (`Mpb.schoolWhy`).
  Study at `roundStart` (`Mpb.levelXp`: the step from the XP threshold reached to the next); a skill learnt sets
  `m.tech[side][ui][k] = round - 1` in the pupil's lane (seen at once, no gold take-back). Destroyed: Engine
  `opts.mpb.watchKills` + `b.watchKill` → `result().killedBy = [{ side, squad, by: { side, squad } }]`; the killer squad
  (lane from its fight id) gets `floor(levelXp / 2)`.
- Game (default off in 1v1): `Game.mpbHeld(sq)` (`sq.jailed || sq.school`): no `canMove`, no `unplaceSquad`, can't carry
  (`boardable`); a School squad can't be sold; `Game.schoolLock(m, side, ui)`: `place` refuses that unit type (and the AI
  skips it). Renderers skip held squads on the board (the building shows them: the jail's window, the school's round
  window). Page: `MPB_SHEET.jail` / `.school` (lists + "tap one on the board" through the new `mpbPicking` hook in
  `mpbTap`), `mpbHeldNote` on a held squad's sheet.
- Framework additions: `Mpb.botTurn(m, side)` (hooks.bot, called from `Duo.botTurn` before `Game.aiDeploy`),
  `Mpb.reconcile(lanes)` (hooks.reconcile, called at the end of `Duo.rebuildTeam`: after Undo all the School's record
  and the lanes' squads agree again), `Mpb.litTiles`.
- Tests: `node tools/tests/mpb-b4.js`.

## To check with Vilhelm

- Jail is "technical" (his text) though the brief's table had it with the defensive ones; 1500 health, 8 armor.
- Jail a squad: a squad, not a single unit; only squads from earlier rounds that the buyer can see (riders can't be
  picked). The jailed team finds out at the fight (until then they may still move or sell it; a squad sold or put in
  their School before the fight refunds the 100). The jailed squad sits out the fight of the round it was jailed in.
- Selling a jailed squad is allowed (half its worth, as always) and frees the jailer's cell (no refund).
- "Freed unit types can't be jailed again": every unit type that was in the jail when it fell, for the rest of the
  match, whoever owns them.
- "Can't be used next round": no jailing, no Skywatch and no guards, but the jail still stands in the fight (a target).
- Skywatch's 3-tile range is a square (the 7 x 7 tiles round it), matching the lit tiles.
- Jail guards: the Keep's 4 Wisps-based guards; they hold until an enemy is in their awareness (or one hits them), then
  fight as Keep guards do. They count as units for winning a round, as the Keep's do.
- School: either player may put in one of their own squads; one squad in all. Not a rider, a carrier with a rider, a
  squad moved this round, or a building. A squad put in this round can't be taken back while inside.
- "A level of XP": the XP between the threshold its XP has reached and the next one (a squad not levelled up yet still
  gets the next step); none at the top level. Levelling up still costs gold. Double study gives two steps' worth.
- A skill learnt in the School is the unit type's (skills are per unit type in this game), so the player's other squads
  of that type get it too; it is free and counts like any bought skill for the price of the next one. One at a time;
  "Stop learning" only in the round it was started.
- The squad inside can't be sold (take it out first, free). Taking it out is hidden from the opponents until the fight
  (they still see it in the School), and so is putting it in (they see it on its tile).
- School destroyed: the squad that landed the killing blow gets half a level's XP (its own next step); the squad inside
  loses all XP and levels it gained inside, including levels bought with gold (no refund), and any skill being learnt;
  skills already learnt stay.
- Tapping the School opens its sheet (with the squad inside and a Sheet button for the squad's own sheet) rather than
  the squad's sheet straight away.
