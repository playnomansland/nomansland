# Group B5: the Shadow Den and the Factory (2v2 buildings)

## Patch notes

- **The Shadow Den** (technical building, 800 health, 2 armor). While your team has it:
  - Rogues, Assassins and Shadowhands placed on a flank tile (an Assassin also in the enemy's zone) don't stand still the 3 s flank delay in their first fight.
  - **Shadowhands** are in your shop (tier 1, 100 gold, no unlock): a Rogue at half health and half damage, 1 body, stealthed. They can go in no man's land.
  - **Shadow deployment:** each of you gets 1 more deployment a round that only a Rogue, an Assassin or a Shadowhands can use.
  - **Impersonate** (200 gold, once a round each): pick an enemy tier 1 or 2 squad you can see. 5 s into the fight one of its bodies becomes a Shadowhand of yours.
  - **Sabotage** (100 gold, once a round for the team): pick a unit type. Next round the other team can't deploy it. They see a big red X on its card.
  - **Insider information** (350 gold, once a round for the team): next round your team sees the other team's deployment live, and their deploy clock is 15 s shorter. They see "You are being watched". If your Den is destroyed in the fight of the round you bought it, they get it for free next round.
  - **If the Den is destroyed:** you lose all your Shadowhands squads (no gold back), and no Den skills the next round. It stands again in the next fight.
- **The Factory** (economic building): the Cog Builder's Factory as a team building. It is built at once, no Cog Builder needed. Each round either of you can buy one of its units for the team: Cogbot Fighter (100), Cogbot Machinegunner (100) or CogMech (300). Whoever buys pays, and the unit shows in their colour. One unit a round per Factory. If it is destroyed in a fight, it is being rebuilt and makes nothing the next round. A Cog Builder's own Factory still works beside it. It flies a big team banner, so you can tell it from a Cog Builder's.

## README lines

- Factory (`factory` region of `src/mpb.js`, `FAC`): hp 1000, armor 0, interior + spacing. Skills `fighter` / `gunner` / `cogmech` (`per: 'round'`, prices = the Cog Builder Factory's building skills, checked by the test); `hooks.canBuy` allows one of the three a round for the record (`Mpb.facNow`) and none the round after it fell (`facResting`). `hooks.bodies` puts `b.produce` on the default structure body from `Game.buildingMakes(m, side, info, sk, 0, FAC.noBuff, null)` (extracted from `Game.buildingBodies`, which now calls it: no change to the Cog Builder's Factory) plus `produce.lane` = the buyer's lane; Engine: a produced body gets `kid.lane = b.produce.lane` (default off: the Cog Builder's produce has no lane). No site, no level, no builder skills, no Overwatch garrison. Looks: `MPB_DRAW.factory` (the Cog factory's `drawFactory` + a team banner), `BUILD['mpb:factory']` (the `factory()` model + eaves band and a tall team banner).
- Shadow Den (`shadowDen` region, `DEN`): hp 800, armor 2, interior + spacing.
  - Shadowhands: the kind field `extraUnits(units)` (framework: `Mpb.extraUnits`, appended by `Duo.unitSet` after the Mechites, so the 2v2 set is built-in + Rollers, Cog Builder, Mechite Guards, Shadowhands) makes it from the set's Rogue: hp/hit x0.5, 1 body, cost 1 (100 gold, tier 1), unlock 0, skills `stealth` + `forward` innate (+ the Rogue's third), `noPack` (never in a starting package), `mpbUnit: 'shadowDen'`, look `mpb: 'shadowDen'`, `mpbSkin: 'hand'` (`MPB_BODY.shadowDen`, `BUILD['mpb:shadowDen:hand']`). `Game.isOff` asks `Mpb.unitOff`: a unit with `mpbUnit` is off for a team without a placed building of that kind (so the shop, the AI, unlocks and the rules leave it out).
  - Shadow deployment: `Game.deploysLeft(m, side, ui)` (new optional `ui`) adds `Mpb.shadowBonus`: a Den unit (`DEN.units`: Rogue, Assassin, Shadowhands) may use `DEN.tokens` (1) more; any other placement only what Den units already took. `Game.place` passes the unit. The page passes it in the shop's `canBuy` and after a placement.
  - No flank delay: `hooks.squadBodies` sets `delay = 0` on a Den unit's bodies exactly where `Game.bodiesFor` gave it the flank delay.
  - Impersonate (`roundPlayer`, args `who` (0: the buyer's own lane's opponent, 1: the other lane's, from `m.team`), `id` (that lane's own squad id)): targets `Mpb.impTargets` = tier <= 2 squads the buyer can see (`Game.foeSeen` / `teamSeen`). `rec.data.imp = [{ round, lane, tl, id }]`; `hooks.battleOpts` sets `opts.mpb.shadowImpersonate = [{ side, squad (fight id: lane 1's + `Duo.OFF`), at: 5, unit (the Shadowhands unit), sq: Mpb.extraOf(k, j), lane }]`. Engine (default off): at `at` s the target squad's first living body (not boarded, not a structure) leaves the fight (`sacrificed`, no kill) and a Shadowhand body of the buyer's side (`tier 1`, `impersonated`, its `lane`) stands in its place. Nothing happens if the squad isn't there.
  - Sabotage (`round`, arg `ui`): `rec.data.sab = { round, ui, lane }`; `Game.mpbLocked(m, side, ui)` (= `Mpb.sabotaged`) is true in the round after for the other team, both lanes: `Game.place` refuses it, `aiDeploy` skips it, the page's shop card shows a red X (`.mpb-x`) and can't be picked.
  - Insider information (`round`): `rec.data.insider = { round }`; `afterFight` turns it into `rec.data.watch = { round: next, by: the buyer (or the other team when the Den fell in that fight), free }`. `Mpb.watching(m, side)`: `Game.foeSeen` / `Game.teamSeen` return the watched team's squads live (current tiles, riders still hidden) and `Duo.viewFor` gives the watchers an unhidden view (`Referee.view` with `deploy` false) of both lanes. `Duo.cutFor(D, seat)` (`Mpb.deployCut`, 15000 ms): `Duo.act` refuses the watched team's actions after `D.deadline - cut + GRACE_MS` ('time'); `server/src/duo.js` sends them `remaining` (and `held`) less the cut. The fight still starts at `D.deadline`. The page: `MPB_SHEET.shadowDen.panel()` ("You are being watched" / "you see their deployment live"), through a new optional `panel()` of `MPB_SHEET` kinds called from `mpbPanel`.
  - Downside: `afterFight` when `rec.fell === round`: every squad of a `mpbUnit: 'shadowDen'` unit of the team is removed in both lanes (no refund, `Mpb.news`), and `denResting` refuses the three skills the next round (recruiting stays).
  - Hidden: `hooks.hide` drops this round's `imp`, `sab` and `insider` from the opponents' view (the framework already hides the clicks).
- Bots: a new framework hook `hooks.bot(m, side, rec, r)` called by `Mpb.bots(m, side, r)` from `Duo.botTurn` before `Game.aiDeploy` (r: a seeded stream from `D.seed`, the round and the seat). The Factory buys a unit 60% of rounds with gold to spare; the Den impersonates (30%), sabotages a type they field (25%) and buys Insider information (15%); `aiDeploy` may recruit Shadowhands.
- Tests: `tools/tests/mpb-b5.js` (15 checks); `tools/tests/duo.js` now expects Shadowhands after the Mechites in the 2v2 set.

## To check with Vilhelm

- Shadowhands are a unit of the 2v2 set that only a team with a Den sees in its shop (recruiting = placing it like any unit, 100 gold, tier 1, no unlock). They keep the Rogue's speed, range, attack speed and Stealth, get Forward planting built in (no man's land), and the Rogue's third skill can be bought for them. They level and sell like any squad (sell: half of 100).
- "Shadow deployment" is read as one extra deployment per player per round, usable only by Rogues, Assassins and Shadowhands; your normal deployments never grow from it.
- "0 deployment time in flanks" covers the Assassin's delay in the enemy's zone too.
- Impersonate's timer: 5 s. The Shadowhand it makes fights only in that fight (it isn't a squad afterwards); the body taken out doesn't count as a kill. Only squads the buyer can see are targets (an enemy squad placed this round is hidden, unless you are watching them with Insider information).
- Sabotage locks the type for both opponents for the next round (placing new squads only; their squads already on the board still fight and move). Any unit type may be picked.
- Insider information shows everything the other team does while deploying (squads, buys, Vault) to both watchers, and their clock (75 s of a 90 s round) runs out 15 s early: after that they can't act but the fight starts at the normal time. "Destroyed while active" is read as: destroyed in the fight of the round it was bought.
- Den destroyed: the Shadowhands squads are removed (no gold back); Shadowhands made by Impersonate during that fight keep fighting to its end. "No Den skills next round" blocks Impersonate, Sabotage and Insider information; recruiting Shadowhands and the Shadow deployment keep working.
- The Factory MPB is not levelled, gets no builder skills (Master engineering etc.) and has no build site: it simply stands. Its downside reading ("as the Cog Builder's"): destroyed in a fight, it makes nothing the next round (the Cog Builder's must be built again).
- The Factory's units fight for the team; their XP and damage count for the Factory, not for the buyer's squads.
